Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

MogDuel ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what the app needs to function:

  • Profile data – display name, age, gender, country you enter.
  • Photos – profile picture and battle photo you choose to upload. These are stored locally on your device.
  • Battle and scan results – ELO score, vote history, scan history. All stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase status – whether you have an active subscription or scan credits, managed by RevenueCat.
  • Device identifiers – used by RevenueCat to associate your purchases with your device.

2. Photos and AI Scanning

When you run a paid AI scan, your photo is sent to OpenAI's API (via GPT-4) for facial analysis. The image is transmitted over a secure HTTPS connection and is not stored by us. OpenAI may retain data per their own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing OpenAI's privacy policy at openai.com. Photos you upload for your profile or battles are stored only on your device and are not sent to any server unless you explicitly publish them in a battle.

3. Purchases and RevenueCat

MogDuel uses RevenueCat to handle subscriptions and consumable purchases. RevenueCat receives your purchase receipt and entitlement status to unlock MogDuel Pro features. RevenueCat does not receive your photos or scan results. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Apple – for App Store payments and account authentication.
  • RevenueCat – for purchase management and entitlement checking.
  • OpenAI – for AI-powered face scans (image only, per scan, no retention by us).

5. Local Storage

All profile data, battle history, scan history, and photos are stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults and your device's local storage. No account creation is required. If you delete the app, all local data is permanently removed.

6. Children's Privacy

MogDuel is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you are a parent and believe your child has used the app, contact us at the address below and we will assist in removing the data.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Edit or delete your profile data at any time in the app.
  • Remove your battle photo to unpublish your battle card.
  • Reset all local progress from Settings → Data → Reset Local Progress.
  • Cancel your subscription from your Apple ID settings.
  • Contact us to request any additional data deletion.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. All API calls use HTTPS/TLS encryption. We do not store raw scan photos on any server.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email us at: nathanielfiska@gmail.com
